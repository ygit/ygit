About me

An engineer.
A builder.
A community person.

I’m based in India, building healthcare voice AI at 100ms. Alongside engineering, I’m a community founder, speaker, and Codex Ambassador—bringing developers together across Apple platforms and AI.

The common thread

I enjoy the engineering beneath a smooth interaction: an API that is easy to use, a conversation that handles a correction, or a debugging tool that makes a difficult failure understandable.

Today that means working across voice-agent behavior, structured extraction, business rules, evaluation, and backend integrations. My healthcare work spans benefits verification, eligibility, and prior authorization workflows, with attention to PHI-sensitive data and review tooling. My model experience includes OpenAI models, Gemini, and Gemma.

Earlier roles gave me experience building SDKs for other developers and leading mobile engineering work. Outside the day job, I bring engineers together through Swift communities and share practical lessons through talks and workshops.

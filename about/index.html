I’m based in Mumbai, building healthcare voice AI at 100ms. Alongside engineering, I’m a community founder, speaker, and Codex Ambassador—bringing developers together across Apple platforms and AI.

The common thread

I enjoy the engineering beneath a smooth interaction: an API that is easy to use, a conversation that handles a correction, or a debugging tool that makes a difficult failure understandable.

Today that means working across voice-agent behavior, structured extraction, business rules, evaluation, and backend integrations. My healthcare work spans benefits verification, eligibility, and prior authorization workflows, with attention to PHI-sensitive data and review tooling. My model experience includes OpenAI models, Gemini, and Gemma.

Earlier roles gave me experience building SDKs for other developers and leading mobile engineering work. Outside the day job, I bring engineers together through Swift communities and share practical lessons through talks and workshops.

From SDKs to applied AI

My earlier work put me close to the teams using the software: debugging their integrations, writing documentation, building demo apps, and making releases easier to adopt. At Haptik, making our own application consume the SDK kept the product and developer interface connected.

That experience carries into AI engineering. An agent needs clear contracts with tools and backend services, visible state, useful diagnostics, and a way to recover when an integration fails. My work now applies those habits to conversations, extraction, evaluation, and the services around them.

I have combined hands-on implementation with team leadership, including starting the Flutter and React Native SDK efforts at 100ms while managing three engineers.
